## Purpose
Use your organization workspace to register devices, understand their readings, and monitor results. The organization selector keeps each workspace separate.

## Before you start
You need an invitation or membership in the organization. Your role determines which pages and actions are available.

## Steps
1. Choose an organization from the selector at the top of the sidebar.
2. Open **Device profiles** to describe what a device reports, then review and publish its profile.
3. Open **Discovery** to inspect a source, or **Devices** to register a device.
4. In the device, configure parameter mappings, review, and activate the configuration.
5. Check **Measurements** and **History**, then build a dashboard or automation when readings are arriving.

## Expected result
The device has an applied configuration and readings you can inspect. A saved draft is an intermediate step; check the activation result.

## Troubleshooting
If a page or button is unavailable, ask your organization administrator to check your role. A connected gateway alone does not mean a device is registered or its configuration is active.
