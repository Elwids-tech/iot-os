## Purpose
**Alerts** records live conditions and the team’s response, including acknowledgment, assignment, and comments.

## Before you start
Viewing requires device viewing permission. Response actions require their respective device management capabilities.

## Steps
1. Open **Alerts** and filter by condition, acknowledgment, device, or workflow.
2. Select an alert to inspect its source, timestamps, evidence, and current condition.
3. Acknowledge it when you have seen it and assign a responsible member where appropriate.
4. Add a comment with your findings.
5. Continue checking the condition until the source reports recovery or clearance.

## Expected result
Acknowledgment records that someone saw the alert; assignment records responsibility. Neither automatically clears its underlying condition.

## Troubleshooting
If an alert remains active after acknowledgment, inspect the latest condition evidence. Notification delivery is separate from the alert record. Check notification settings when recipients do not receive an update.
