## Purpose
A brand makes the portal yours for the people you serve: your product name, logo and icon on the sign-in page, the loading screen and the browser tab. An address is where they open it, under your name.

## Before you start
You need to own the billing account, and its plan must include branding. Have a logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 1 MB) and a square icon of at least 512 × 512 pixels.

## Steps
1. **Open the billing account.** Open your user menu, choose **Billing accounts**, and find **Branding**.
2. **Set the brand.** Enter a product name and, if you like, a short name and a support link. Upload the logo and the icon; add a second logo for dark appearance if yours is hard to read on a dark background. The preview shows the sign-in card as your people will see it. Select **Save**.
3. **Claim an address.** Under **Addresses**, enter a name of 3 to 40 letters, numbers and hyphens and select **Claim address**. It works at once.
4. **Share the address.** Give it to your people. Only members of this billing account's organizations can sign in there, and they see only those organizations.
5. **Change or remove it.** Enter a new name and select **Change address**: the old name sends people to the new one for 30 days. **Remove** stops the address within a minute; the name is held for you for 30 days.
6. **Give an organization its own logo.** As an administrator of the organization, open **Settings**, then **General**, and upload a logo. It shows in the sidebar for that organization. Without one, the organization shows the billing account's logo.

## Expected result
At your address, the sign-in page, loading screen and browser tab carry your brand from the first moment. Inside the portal each organization shows its own logo, or yours.

## Troubleshooting
- **The brand cannot be edited.** Branding is not part of the billing account's plan. Contact the platform operator.
- **The name is taken or reserved.** Another account holds it, or released it less than 30 days ago, or it is one the platform keeps for itself. Choose another.
- **The name field is disabled.** Save a product name first: the address shows it on its sign-in page.
- **An image is refused.** The message says why: the file type, more than 1 MB, or an icon that is not square or is smaller than 512 pixels.
- **Someone cannot sign in at the address.** After a right password they see that the account cannot sign in at this address. They are not a member of one of this billing account's organizations: invite them to one.
- **The address says it is not in use.** It was removed, suspended by the platform operator, or branding left the plan. Your saved brand and address are kept and return with the plan.
