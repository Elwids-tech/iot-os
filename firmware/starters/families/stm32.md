# STM32 notes

- **Framework**: STM32Cube (HAL and middleware) generated by STM32CubeMX or STM32CubeIDE,
  usually with FreeRTOS (CMSIS-RTOS v2).
- **Network**: boards with Ethernet use lwIP from STM32Cube. Wi-Fi or cellular often
  comes from a module with its own sockets (level 1) or its own MQTT (level 2).
- **TLS**: mbedTLS from STM32Cube middleware, over lwIP sockets for the stream port. Give
  it a hardware random number generator (RNG peripheral) as its entropy source.
- **Clock**: SNTP from lwIP's apps, or the RTC once it is set. Report the time as invalid
  until it has been set.
- **Storage**: a reserved flash sector, written with the HAL flash driver. Keep bdSeq in
  its own page so wear stays low, or use a small external EEPROM.
- **Lock**: a FreeRTOS mutex (`xSemaphoreCreateMutex`).
- **Traps**:
  - STM32Cube's MQTT or cloud middleware packages can bundle another coreMQTT. Leave them
    out of the build.
  - mbedTLS needs more heap than the default FreeRTOS heap: raise `configTOTAL_HEAP_SIZE`
    and check the TLS buffer sizes.
  - Check that HAL tick and FreeRTOS tick use different timers.
