Pricing plan mockups

Spec 028. Low-fidelity layouts of the screens the spec describes, to agree on content before planning. The real screens use the design system's CollectionPage, DataTable and Panel. Plan names, organization names and numbers are placeholders, not proposed tiers.

User Story 1, FR-001 to FR-006. One plan per tier, each with an alert and an SMS allowance. Archived plans stay on their accounts but cannot be newly assigned.
